Aim and Scope

Krishi Siksha – English Version is a peer-oriented tri-monthly publication dedicated to disseminating scientific knowledge, groundbreaking innovations, and emerging technologies in agriculture and allied sciences, while simultaneously addressing pressing environmental and sustainability challenges of global importance. The publication serves as an interdisciplinary platform that encourages the exchange of scientific ideas, research advancements, practical experiences, and policy-oriented perspectives among researchers, scientists, academicians, students, extension professionals, progressive farmers, agri-entrepreneurs, and members of the broader scientific community. Emphasizing both theoretical advancements and field-level applicability, the magazine highlights contemporary developments in crop science, horticulture, plant biotechnology, soil science, climate-resilient agriculture, precision farming, sustainable resource management, environmental conservation, and digital agriculture.

In addition, Krishi Siksha – English Version actively promotes agri-tech and agri-based start-ups by providing opportunities to showcase innovative technologies, entrepreneurial success stories, business models, and transformative ideas that contribute to sustainable agricultural development and rural empowerment. By integrating scientific communication with practical innovation, the publication aims to nurture creativity, technological advancement, and entrepreneurial spirit among young researchers and professionals worldwide. Through its diverse, evidence-based, and knowledge-enriched content, Krishi Siksha – English Version strives to foster academic excellence, strengthen scientific collaboration, encourage sustainable agricultural practices, and inspire a progressive, resilient, and environmentally responsible future for global agriculture and allied sectors.

Subject Areas

Krishi Siksha – English Version welcomes original articles, review papers, technical notes, case studies, innovative concepts, and interdisciplinary contributions related to agriculture, allied sciences, environmental sustainability, and emerging technologies. The major subject areas include, but are not limited to, the following:

1. Crop Production and Crop Science

  • Cereals and grain crops
  • Pulses and legumes
  • Fruits and vegetable crops
  • Floriculture and ornamental crops
  • Fibre crops including cotton
  • Integrated crop management
  • Cropping systems and diversification

2. Crop Protection

  • Integrated pest management (IPM)
  • Plant pathology and disease management
  • Weed science and invasive species management
  • Biological control and biopesticides
  • Nematology and plant health protection

3. Plant Breeding, Genetics, and Biotechnology

  • Conventional and molecular breeding
  • Genetic diversity and germplasm conservation
  • Mutation breeding and genomic approaches
  • Marker-assisted selection and gene editing
  • Plant tissue culture and synthetic seed technology

4. Crop Nutrition, Physiology, and Irrigation

  • Plant nutrition and nutrient management
  • Fertilizer management and nutrient-use efficiency
  • Crop physiology and stress biology
  • Irrigation scheduling and water management
  • Precision nutrient and irrigation management

5. Sustainable and Climate-Resilient Agriculture

  • Precision agriculture and smart farming
  • Conservation agriculture
  • Organic and natural farming
  • Ecological and regenerative agriculture
  • Climate-smart agricultural technologies
  • Carbon sequestration and sustainable resource management

6. Soil Science and Water Management

  • Soil physics and soil chemistry
  • Soil microbiology and soil biodiversity
  • Soil fertility and health management
  • Soil and water conservation
  • Irrigation and water-use efficiency
  • Water quality and watershed management

7. Environmental and Climate Change Studies

  • Environmental impacts on agricultural productivity
  • Climate change adaptation and mitigation
  • Agroecology and ecosystem services
  • Pollution management and environmental restoration
  • Sustainable environmental stewardship

8. Animal Production and Veterinary Sciences

  • Livestock and poultry production systems
  • Animal breeding and genetics
  • Animal nutrition and feed technology
  • Dairy and integrated farming systems
  • Animal health and welfare

9. Rural Management and Agricultural Development

  • Agricultural economics and agribusiness
  • Rural livelihoods and community development
  • Agricultural extension and rural innovation
  • Trade, policy, and food security
  • Entrepreneurship and start-up development in agriculture

10. Agricultural Engineering and Emerging Technologies

  • Farm machinery and mechanization
  • Artificial Intelligence (AI) in agriculture
  • Internet of Things (IoT) and smart agriculture
  • Remote sensing and geospatial technologies
  • Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
  • Data analytics and digital agriculture
  • Drone technology and automation

11. Agricultural Product Quality, Health, and Safety

  • Post-harvest management and value addition
  • Food quality and safety standards
  • Product storage and freshness maintenance
  • Plant and animal quarantine/inspection
  • Supply chain and cold-chain management

12. Interdisciplinary and Emerging Areas

  • Waste management and circular bioeconomy
  • Renewable energy in agriculture
  • Urban and peri-urban agriculture
  • Hydroponics, aeroponics, and soilless cultivation
  • Agri-tech innovations and start-up ecosystems
  • Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) in agriculture

The Editorial Board also encourages submissions on other emerging and interdisciplinary topics that contribute to the advancement of sustainable agriculture, environmental resilience, food security, and technological innovation.

Aim and Scope

Krishi Siksha – English Version is a peer-oriented tri-monthly publication devoted to disseminating scientific knowledge, innovative research, emerging technologies, and sustainable advancements in agriculture and allied sciences. The publication aims to bridge the gap between scientific research, field-level application, and policy-oriented perspectives by providing a multidisciplinary platform for researchers, academicians, scientists, students, extension workers, progressive farmers, and agri-entrepreneurs. It emphasizes contemporary developments in crop science, horticulture, plant biotechnology, soil science, environmental sustainability, climate-resilient agriculture, precision farming, artificial intelligence in agriculture, agri-business management, and other frontier areas of agricultural innovation. In addition, the magazine actively promotes agri-tech and agri-based start-ups by highlighting their scientific approaches, innovative business models, technological interventions, and entrepreneurial success stories, thereby fostering a culture of innovation, scientific temperament, and sustainable rural development. Through evidence-based articles, reviews, technical insights, and knowledge-sharing initiatives, Krishi Siksha – English Version strives to encourage scientific communication, promote environmentally responsible agricultural practices, and contribute toward the development of a resilient, technology-driven, and sustainable global agricultural system.

Guidelines for Authors and Publication Policy

All submitted manuscripts undergo a rigorous editorial and peer-review process conducted by the Editorial Board and designated subject experts to ensure scientific quality, originality, clarity, and relevance. Manuscripts that fail to comply with the prescribed submission format, ethical standards, or publication guidelines will not be considered for review or publication. Where necessary, submitted chapters may be returned to the authors for revision following detailed evaluation by reviewers and subject specialists. Final acceptance is granted only after satisfactory revision, technical editing, and approval by the Editorial Board. Authors must ensure that the submitted work is original, unpublished, and free from plagiarism or copyright infringement. Upon acceptance of the manuscript, the copyright of the published material will be transferred to the publisher. For any queries related to manuscript preparation, formatting, or submission procedures, authors are encouraged to contact the Editorial Board directly.

The Editorial Board of Krishi Siksha cordially invites scholarly, innovative, and scientifically sound contributions for upcoming issues. Authors are requested to carefully follow the instructions provided below to facilitate smooth review, editing, and publication of their chapters.

Author Guidelines

1. Compliance with Guidelines

Authors must strictly follow all submission and formatting guidelines. Every instruction mentioned herein is important and mandatory for maintaining publication quality and uniformity.

2. Publication with ISBN

All accepted chapters will be published in a compiled volume/book carrying a registered ISBN number, ensuring academic credibility, wider dissemination, and long-term accessibility.

3. No Publication Charges

There are no submission, processing, or publication charges for selected manuscripts. Publication in Krishi Siksha is entirely free of cost for authors.

4. Selection Criteria

Each issue will publish a limited number of high-quality chapters. Selection will be based on scientific merit, originality, innovation, clarity, relevance to agriculture and allied sciences, and recommendations of reviewers and the Editorial Board.

5. Submission Format

Manuscripts must be submitted only in Microsoft Word (.doc/.docx) format through the official Email or WhatsApp contact provided by the Editorial Board. Improperly formatted or incomplete submissions will not be considered.

6. Page Limit

The chapter length should generally be within 8–10 pages, including title, author information, abstract, tables, figures, photographs, and references.

7. Structure of the Chapter

Each manuscript should contain the following sections in proper sequence:

  • Title
  • Author details
  • Abstract (maximum 200 words)
  • Keywords
  • Introduction
  • Main content with suitable headings/subheadings
  • Conclusion
  • Acknowledgment (if applicable)
  • References

8. Quality and Presentation

Articles should be informative, scientifically accurate, innovative, and aligned with current developments in agriculture and allied sciences. Authors are strongly encouraged to include:

  • Tables
  • Graphs
  • Illustrations
  • High-quality photographs (300–500 dpi)

Figure captions must be written below the figures and table captions above the tables using proper numbering format, such as:

Fig. 1: Caption of the figure
Table 1: Caption of the table

All figures and tables must be properly cited and discussed within the main text.

9. Chapter Title Formatting

The title of the chapter should be concise, informative, and clearly representative of the manuscript content.

  • Font: Times New Roman
  • Size: 14 pt
  • Style: Bold
  • Recommended length: 15–18 words

10. Author Information

The following details must be provided below the title:

  • Full name(s) of author(s)
  • Complete institutional affiliation(s)
  • Full postal address(es)
  • Email address of the corresponding author

Formatting requirements:

  • Font: Times New Roman
  • Size: 12 pt
  • Corresponding author email: Italicized

11. Plagiarism Policy

Submitted manuscripts must have a similarity index below 15%. Authors are solely responsible for ensuring originality and addressing any plagiarism-related concerns. Manuscripts containing significant plagiarism or unethical content will be rejected immediately.

12. Disclosure of AI Usage

Authors must disclose the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)-assisted tools, if any, in the acknowledgment section. This includes AI-generated:

  • Text
  • Images
  • Figures
  • Graphs
  • Codes or datasets

The disclosure must clearly mention:

  • Name of the AI tool/system used
  • Sections where AI assistance was applied
  • Extent and purpose of its use

Use of AI for grammar or language improvement should also preferably be disclosed.

13. Text Formatting

  • Main text: Times New Roman, 12 pt
  • Main headings: 14 pt, bold
  • Subheadings: 12 pt, bold
  • Line spacing: 1.5

Authors should avoid:

  • Page borders
  • Page numbers
  • Multiple separate files

All content including text, figures, tables, and references must be merged into a single compiled Word document before submission.

14. Language and Writing Style

The manuscript should be written in clear, concise, and grammatically correct English that is easy to understand while maintaining scientific accuracy and professionalism.

15. Units and Scientific Nomenclature

Authors should follow internationally accepted scientific standards:

  • Use SI (Metric) units throughout the manuscript
  • Define all abbreviations at first mention
  • Mention sources of datasets clearly
  • Scientific terminology, symbols, abbreviations, and nomenclature should conform to recommendations of:
    • International Union of Biochemistry (IUB)
    • International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry (IUPAC)

16. Reference Style

In-text Citation: Use author-year citation style:

  • Single author: X (2025)
  • Two authors: X and Y (2025)
  • Multiple authors: X et al. (2025)

Bibliography: References must follow APA format and DOI numbers should be included wherever available.
Example:
Zhao, D., Chen, Y., Yuan, H., & Chen, D. (2025). Life cycle optimization oriented to sustainable waste management and circular economy: A review. Waste Management, 191, 89–106. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wasman.2024.11.001

17. Author Responsibility

Authors bear complete responsibility for:

  • Scientific accuracy
  • Originality of the manuscript
  • Ethical compliance
  • Copyright permissions
  • Plagiarism-related issues

The views expressed in the articles are solely those of the authors.

18. Soft Copy of Publication

A digital copy (PDF) of the published volume will be shared with all contributing authors after publication.

19. Hard Copy Availability

Only the September–October Issue will be published in printed format. Hard copies may be obtained from the: Agriculture Information Technology Centre (ATIC), Department of Agriculture, Brainware University.

20. Re-submission Policy

Authors are requested not to re-submit manuscripts or topics that have previously been rejected in earlier issues of Krishi Siksha unless substantial modifications and improvements have been made.

What is ORCID?

An ORCID iD is a nonproprietary alphanumeric code that uniquely identifies an academic author. It is a 16-digit code, in the format: 0000-0000-0000-000X. Publishers use it to unambiguously attribute any published work to the correct authors.

ORCID is a not-for-profit association of publishers and academic organizations that maintains the central registry of these personal identifiers (ORCID iDs) and associated public profiles for authors in academic publishing. Please visit http://orcid.org/ for more information.

Login Help

Below are some suggestions that may help if you're having trouble logging in to the system.

What if I forgot my Username and/or Password?

If you forgot your username and/or password, the system can send you an email that contains your Login Details. On the Login page, click the Send Login Details link.

  • Enter your email address and click the Send Login Details button. (If more than one account in the system is associated with your email address, you will be asked to enter your name also.)
  • If the system finds a record matching your information, you will receive an email containing your username and a link to change your password. Click the link and follow instructions on the page to create a new password.

Note: If you enter an invalid username/password combination 5 times within 5 minutes, your account will be locked and will not be available for 10 minutes after the fifth failed login attempt. During the lockout period, you may click the Send Login Details link and follow the steps above to recover your username and reset your password.

How do I change my Password?

(My current password is hard to remember, and I keep forgetting it each time I try to log in to the system.)

  • To change your password, log in to the system using your current Username and Password.
  • In the top navigation menu, click Update My Information.
  • In the top section of the page (Login Information), enter a new password in the Password field.
  • Enter the new password again in the Re-type Password field. For security purposes, your password will always display on this page as asterisks in both fields.
  • Click the Submit button at the bottom of the page to save your changes.

What does 'Or Login via:' mean?

This feature, sometimes referred to as "Single Sign-On," enables you to log in to this publication's Editorial Manager site using your credentials from another site. Clicking any icon in this section tells the system to ask that third party to establish your identity through the user account you have on that site. To log in using this method:

  • Do not fill in the Username or Password fields.
  • Instead, click on the external site's icon to trigger Single Sign-On using that site. This opens the login function for the external site. (This step might be skipped if you are already signed in to the external system.)
  • Sign in to the external system.
  • Depending on the system, you may be asked to give Editorial Manager access to your profile in order to return any ID needed to locate your user record. Allow this authentication.

Note: In order to use single sign-on functionality, you must have a user record in Editorial Manager and the record must be linked to the third-party system by you. If you attempt to log in with single sign-on, Editorial Manager will ask you to register if it can’t find a linked record, and will then link the external site to your user record. Each external system only needs to be linked once in order for you to use it for single sign-on.

How do I know which login button to select on the Login Page?

The buttons enable you to log into the system with a particular Role. If you are not sure which role you have, contact the publication office. Below is a synopsis of the function each role performs.

  • Author: Make a new or revised submission, Edit or Approve a submission, Check the status of a submission
  • Editor: Perform editorial office functions, Perform editorial tasks, Run performance reports, Access System Administration functions
  • Reviewer: Respond to a Reviewer Invitation, Submit a Reviewer Recommendation on a submission
  • Publisher: Perform production tasks

I serve as a Reviewer on several publications. Why can't I log into this publication as a Reviewer?

Even though you serve as a Reviewer for other publications, you may not be assigned a Reviewer role on this publication. Click Contact Us in the top navigation menu to send a message to the editorial staff if you want to request a Reviewer role.
